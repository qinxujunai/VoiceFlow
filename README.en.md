## One small capsule. From a thought to a word.

![VoiceFlow product demo](docs/voiceflow-demo.svg)

Place your cursor in a text field. Press **F2** to start and again to finish.
Your voice becomes text on your computer. Recording and recognition stay local.

## Your words. Your habits.

Skip the final period in a chat. Keep punctuation when writing. Choose the ending that suits you.
Your dictation stays in local history, ready to revisit and copy.
